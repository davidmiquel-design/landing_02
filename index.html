A silent MASS — CARVED by SHADOW, revealed by the GLOW OF the NIGHT*

* Since 1987 SHENKEN has built 64 buildings in raw, board-formed concrete across 11 countries — each one designed to be read after dark.

( The studio )

SHENKEN designs in raw, board-formed concrete. For almost four decades we have drawn houses, baths, chapels and archives meant to be read at night — when mass turns into silhouette and light becomes a building material.

SELECTED ( ↓ ) WORKS

( Selected works, 2016 — 2024 )

Six buildings, ONE MATERIAL

  1. 01

    Casa Umbra

    Private house · Menorca, ES · 2021

    A single concrete beam cantilevered fourteen metres over the sea. One ribbon window, one horizon.

  2. 02

    The Nocturne Baths

    Thermal baths · Asturias, ES · 2023

    A bathhouse carved out of darkness, where blades of moonlight fall on still water.

  3. 03

    Archive of Silence

    Public library · Berlin, DE · 2019

    An inverted ziggurat of reading rooms, each floor stepping out over the wet plaza below.

  4. 04

    Chapel of the Slit

    Chapel · Soria, ES · 2016

    One wall, one opening, one line of light. Nothing else was allowed in.

  5. 05

    Torre Brava

    Housing tower · Porto, PT · 2024

    Ninety-six homes stacked like quarried stone, their balconies shifting with the wind.

  6. 06

    Museum of Erosion

    Museum · Lanzarote, ES · 2022

    A floating roof slab over black lava, and a pool that keeps the moon.
